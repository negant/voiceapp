Summary

Voice App processes audio on your device for Basic and Advanced effects. Optional AI voice effects send audio to a cloud provider. We do not require a user account. We use a random device identifier for credits and abuse prevention.

Data we process

Data Purpose Where
Audio (import, recording, export) Local voice effects; save on device Device (SQLite / app storage)
Audio for AI effects Cloud voice conversion (configured STS) Transiently with the AI provider
Device ID (random UUID) Credit balance, free grant, support Device and Negant API
Plan / credit ledger Free vs Premium, AI minute usage API database
App version / platform Setup, force update, diagnostics API (no PII)
Store purchase tokens Verify Premium subscription API

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or precise location as part of the core product.

Permissions (Android / iOS)

  • Microphone — only when you choose Record.
  • Read audio / media — only when you import a selected file.
  • Internet — credits API, optional AI, update checks.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

  • Contract / requested service — processing audio and credits you ask for.
  • Legitimate interest — Device ID to prevent free-tier abuse; API security.

Third parties

  • Negant API — devices, credits, setup.
  • AI provider — audio only when you run an AI effect.
  • Google Play / App Store — Premium payments under store policies.

Retention

  • Local audio and presets: until you delete them or clear app data.
  • Device credit records: as long as needed for the service and abuse prevention.
  • Purchase verification: as required for billing and renewals.

Your choices

  • Use Basic/Advanced without AI — audio stays on device.
  • Decline microphone / media permissions — related features stay unavailable.
  • Manage or cancel Premium in your store subscriptions.
  • Contact support with your Device ID (Settings → About).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
